## Mapping principles

1. **One primary hypothesis per page** (title carries it, H1 reinforces, body
   answers it) — no keyword stuffing, no two pages targeting the same term.
2. **Honesty filter:** never target keywords for capabilities the product lacks
   (e.g. "meta ads manager", "tiktok dm automation", "free unlimited whatsapp
   sender"). The `/integrations` page labels simulated connectors — content
   must never outrank that honesty.
3. **Transactional pages** (`/pricing`, `/signup`) target commercial terms;
   **informational pages** (`/docs`, `/faq`, `/changelog`) target long-tail
   questions and earn links.
4. **Arabic pages** are full translations (not machine stubs) — they can rank
   on their own; keep `hreflang` en↔ar pairs intact when adding pages.

## Content backlog (hypothesis-driven, not scheduled)

1. `/docs` deep-dives: WhatsApp 24h window, comment-to-DM rules, eligibility
   verdicts — each answers one long-tail question completely.
2. Changelog entries as indexable product news (already shipping).
3. Use-case playbooks: "abandoned cart recovery with WhatsApp" (e-commerce),
   "client onboarding template" (agencies) — only after the workflows exist
   as shareable templates.
4. Comparison content ONLY if honest: "FlowPilot AI vs generic chatbot
   builders" must disclose simulated vs real integrations.

## What NOT to do

- No fake search-volume numbers in reports or plans.
- No doorway pages, no auto-generated city/industry pages.
- No buying links; no keyword-stuffed footers.
- No claiming "official Meta partner" status — we use official APIs; that is
  the exact claim and no more.
